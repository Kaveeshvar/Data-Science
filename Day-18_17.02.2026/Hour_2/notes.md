1️⃣ Concept Clarity

A) LTV estimation per cohort (SQL-first reality)

Cohort definition

Signup week

First successful transaction week (activation cohort) ✅ best for wallets

KYC completion week (sometimes)


In fintech, your cohort LTV must be segmented:

by acquisition channel (paid vs organic)

by MCC mix (gaming-heavy cohorts behave wildly differently)

by risk flags (device, geo, velocity)


B) CAC vs Revenue per acquisition channel (stop worshipping CAC)
The question isn’t “which CAC is low?”
It’s: Which channel pays back and which is subsidized?

You want at least these comparisons:

CAC per activated user (not signup)

30/90-day revenue (or CM) per activated user

LTV:CAC ratio and Payback period proxy

Cohort drift (is last month’s channel still as good?)
